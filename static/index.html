Data Mining Pipeline Specifications

  • Stage 3 & 4 Missingness Handling: Missing Not At Random (MNAR) pattern preserved with 6 binary indicator flags (e.g. Tenure_was_missing, WarehouseToHome_was_missing) + median imputation.
  • Stage 4 Category Consolidation: Mapped typographical synonyms (Phone → Mobile Phone, CC → Credit Card, COD → Cash on Delivery).
  • Stage 7 Feature Engineering: Engineered critical threshold flags (Tenure_New_0_3mo and Tenure_Loyal_12mo_plus) that capture the non-linear onboarding cliff.
  • Imbalance Strategy: Trained with class weighting and threshold tuning to optimize the minority churn recall (81.93%) without sacrificing precision (90.56%).
